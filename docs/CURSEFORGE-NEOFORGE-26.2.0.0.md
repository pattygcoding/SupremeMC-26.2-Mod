# SupremeMC 26.2.0.0 — NeoForge

Expand your Minecraft world with SupremeMC, a vanilla-inspired content mod for **Minecraft 26.2**. This NeoForge release adds new places to explore, materials to progress through, useful crops and foods, and distinctive creatures while keeping the familiar feel of Minecraft.

## Highlights

- Explore new biomes, including Florida Plains, Cays, and Ice Caves.
- Travel to the Icether and Skylands dimensions, each with its own portal and terrain.
- Discover new ores and materials, then craft expanded tool, armor, and building-material progression.
- Grow and harvest cotton, corn, grapes, tomatoes, coconuts, and other plants; cook new foods and brew new potions.
- Encounter Fire Creepers, Snow Creepers, Ender Spiders, and other custom world and mob features.
- Use custom enchantments, utility blocks, and gameplay additions throughout your survival world.

## Installation

Install **Minecraft 26.2** and **NeoForge 26.2.0.1-beta or newer**. Put the SupremeMC NeoForge `.jar` in your `mods` folder and launch the game with the NeoForge profile.

This file is the **NeoForge** build. Do not use it with Fabric.

## Compatibility

- Minecraft: 26.2
- Mod version: 26.2.0.0
- Mod loader: NeoForge
- Java: 25 or newer
