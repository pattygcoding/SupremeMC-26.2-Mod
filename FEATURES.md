# Implemented Features

## Aquamarine Content

- Aquamarine ore generates in ocean biomes, replacing stone, deepslate or tuff, and dripstone with a diamond-like vein profile.
- Aquamarine ore and deepslate aquamarine ore require an iron-tier pickaxe, drop Aquamarine with Fortune support, and award 3-7 experience unless mined with Silk Touch.
- Both ore variants can be smelted or blasted into Aquamarine.
- Nine Aquamarine items craft into one Block of Aquamarine, and the block can be crafted back into nine items.
- Aquamarine Blocks can be used as beacon bases, and Aquamarine can pay for beacon effects.
- Aquamarine pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, sword, helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots are registered with diamond-tier material properties.
- The Aquamarine shovel converts dirt, coarse dirt, and rooted dirt into mud.
- The Aquamarine hoe converts dirt and grass blocks into fully moisturized Wet Farmland.
- Wet Farmland does not lose moisture through random ticks and uses Minecraft's vanilla moist-farmland appearance.
- Wearing a complete Aquamarine armour set continuously grants Water Breathing.
- Equipped Aquamarine armour uses custom Aquamarine equipment textures for adult humanoids, baby humanoids, and leggings.

## Loader Support

- Aquamarine content appears in its own **SupremeMC** creative tab (not the vanilla Ingredients tab) on both Fabric and NeoForge.
- Models, blockstates, loot tables, recipes, and language data are generated by the NeoForge data generator.
- Fabric packages the same generated JSON resources, keeping content data identical between both loaders.
